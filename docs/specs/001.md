## Purpose
This enterprise standard defines the mandatory requirements, governance structures, data schema, operational processes, and compliance obligations for the Digital Asset Register. The register serves as the authoritative source of truth for all digital assets that store, process, or transmit organisational information.  

The standard ensures alignment with ISO 27001:2022, supports risk management, enables audit readiness, and provides a foundation for secure, reliable, and well‑governed digital operations.

---

## Scope
This standard applies to all digital assets owned, operated, or managed by the organisation, including but not limited to:

- Applications (web, desktop, mobile)  
- Databases and data stores  
- APIs, microservices, and integration endpoints  
- Virtual machines, containers, and serverless functions  
- Cloud services (SaaS, PaaS, IaaS)  
- Identity and authentication systems  
- Networked digital infrastructure (firewalls, switches, routers)  
- Data pipelines, ETL processes, and analytics platforms  
- Digital records, datasets, and structured/unstructured information  
- Source code repositories and development environments  

All business units, departments, and external service providers must comply with this standard.

---

## Objectives
The Digital Asset Register must:

1. Provide a complete and accurate inventory of digital assets.  
2. Support risk assessment, security classification, and control implementation.  
3. Enable lifecycle management, including review, change control, and retirement.  
4. Ensure accountability through clear ownership and custodianship.  
5. Support compliance with ISO 27001:2022 and other regulatory obligations.  
6. Facilitate audit, incident response, and business continuity planning.  

---

## Definitions
Digital Asset  
Any information system component, dataset, or digital service that stores, processes, or transmits information.

Asset Owner  
The individual accountable for the asset’s security, classification, and lifecycle.

Custodian  
The technical administrator responsible for maintaining the asset.

CIA Classification  
Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability ratings applied to information assets.

Criticality  
The business impact resulting from asset failure or compromise.

---

## Mandatory Requirements

5.1 Asset Identification
All digital assets must be uniquely identifiable and recorded in the register.

5.2 Ownership
Each asset must have an assigned Asset Owner and Technical Custodian.

5.3 Classification
All assets must be classified according to confidentiality, integrity, and availability requirements.

5.4 Lifecycle Management
Assets must include review dates, change history, and end‑of‑life status.

5.5 Security Controls
Assets must document applied controls, vulnerabilities, and risk ratings.

5.6 Documentation
Assets must link to relevant policies, procedures, and audit evidence.

5.7 Completeness
No digital asset may operate in production without being recorded in the register.

5.8 Accuracy
The register must be kept accurate and updated within ten business days of any change.

## Governance

7.1 Roles and Responsibilities

Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)  
Responsible for overall governance of the Digital Asset Register and ensuring compliance with this standard.

Enterprise Architecture Function  
Responsible for maintaining the structure, taxonomy, and data model of the register.

Asset Owners  
Accountable for the accuracy, classification, and lifecycle of their assets.

Technical Custodians  
Responsible for maintaining technical attributes, configurations, and operational details.

Internal Audit  
Responsible for verifying compliance with this standard.

---

7.2 Review Requirements
- All digital assets must be reviewed annually.  
- High‑risk assets must be reviewed every six months.  
- Reviews must confirm accuracy, classification, and control effectiveness.  

---

7.3 Change Management
Any change to a digital asset must be reflected in the register as part of the organisation’s change management process.  
Changes include:  
- Version upgrades  
- Configuration changes  
- Migration to new environments  
- Ownership changes  
- Retirement or decommissioning  

---

7.4 Access Control
Only authorised personnel may modify the register.  
Read‑only access must be available to auditors, risk managers, and relevant business units.

---

7.5 Audit
The register must be available for internal and external audits.  
Audit findings related to asset inventory must be remediated within agreed timelines.
