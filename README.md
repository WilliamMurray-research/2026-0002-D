The Unified Asset Registry is the governed, canonical repository for all formal, operational, and conceptual artefacts produced across the research ecosystem. It consolidates every foundational component — including standards, schemas, templates, compilers, DSLs, proofs, algorithms, and structural frameworks — into a single, unified, audit‑ready archive.

This registry serves as the operational backbone of the broader programme. It enforces structural invariants, preserves provenance, and maintains coherence across all projects and domains. Each asset is catalogued, versioned, and integrated within a consistent architectural framework designed for long‑arc stability, reproducible governance, and due‑diligence clarity.

## **Asset Classes**

The Unified Asset Registry contains multiple classes of governed artefacts. Each class will be hyperlinked once directories are instantiated.

### **Standards & Governance**  
Operational standards, structural invariants, ISO‑aligned templates, and governance documents defining the rules and constraints of the ecosystem.

### **Schemas & Templates**  
Formal schemas, canonical templates, and structural scaffolds used across all projects to ensure consistency and reproducibility.

### **Compilers & DSLs**  
Custom compilers, domain‑specific languages, and formal systems developed to support symbolic reasoning, deterministic pipelines, and operator‑theoretic experimentation.

### **Algorithms**  
Canonical algorithms, operator‑theoretic procedures, symbolic routines, and computational methods used across the research roadmap.

### **Proofs & Papers**  
Formal proofs, theoretical documents, conceptual frameworks, and research papers forming the intellectual backbone of the programme.

### **Frameworks & Structural Systems**  
Architectural frameworks, operator models, and structural systems that support the Unified Operator Architecture and related projects.

### **Asset Register**  
A governed index of all artefacts, including provenance, versioning, classification, and structural metadata.

## **Governance Model**

The Unified Asset Registry operates under a strict ISO aligned governance model:

- **Structural invariants** define mandatory directory and file layouts  
- **Versioning rules** ensure reproducibility and auditability  
- **Provenance tracking** records conceptual origin, modifications, and lineage  
- **Cross‑domain coherence** ensures assets integrate cleanly across symbolic, computational, and dynamical systems  
- **Operator‑tier alignment** maintains consistency with the Unified Operator Architecture  

This governance model ensures that every artefact is structurally sound, reproducible, and aligned with the long‑arc trajectory of the research ecosystem.
